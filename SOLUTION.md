# Published solution for JSP-000444

- **Paper:** N. Alon and V. Rödl, *Sharp bounds for some multicolor Ramsey numbers*, Combinatorica **25** (2005), 125–141.
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-005-0011-9
- **Author-hosted PDF (final version):** https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nalon/PDFS/arodl4.pdf
- **Relevant results:**
  - The abstract and Theorem 3.2 with k = 2 give r(K₃, K₃, K_m) = Θ̃(m³), i.e. m³·polylog(m). The paper says this solves "in a strong form" the Erdős–Sós conjecture R(3,3,m)/R(3,m) → ∞ (its Conjecture 1.1).
  - The lower bound uses Section 2 (Theorem 2.1, Lemma 2.2) and Lemma 3.1.
  - The upper bound is in the proof of Theorem 3.2.
- **Problem source:**
  - erdosproblems.com/553: "This was solved by Alon and Rödl [AlRo05], who in fact show that R(3,3,n) ≍ n³(log n)^{O(1)}".
  - The catalog entry JSP-000444 cites [AlRo05] and [Sh83] and marks the problem Solved.
